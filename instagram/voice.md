## Who we are

- **Name:** Videos Gafos
- **Handle:** @videosgafos (Instagram), "Videos Gafos" on YouTube Shorts (cross-posts)
- **What we do, in one sentence:** Short, silly Reels for people who want to laugh a
  lot, in English and Spanish, made from free legal footage with our own jokes,
  voiceover, captions and sound.

  moment, never a real person's dignity.

  a video to a friend or the family group when it hits.
  - 40% Venezuelans (in Venezuela and abroad, especially Miami): Venezuelan slang,

  - 30% US Latinos, many nationalities, bilingual: family, Spanglish, "eso le pasó a
    mi tía". Spanish, Spanglish, English.

    misunderstandings. English.

## The format (since 2026-10-31)

- Real footage that is free and legally reusable: public domain, CC0, CC BY (credited),
  NASA (public domain, with a no-endorsement line), or stock licensed for commercial
  social use. Sources and download tricks: footage-sourcing memory and
  `/mnt/project-files/tools/reel-footage/`.
- Light edits: cuts, 9:16 crop, zooms, freeze-frames, speed ramps, reverse, burned-in
  captions, in-house SFX and music, original clip audio removed.
- Free synthetic voiceover: Kokoro TTS built-in voices (Apache 2.0). Spanish `ef_dora`
  (narrator, the tía) and `em_alex` (deadpan male), English `am_michael` (mission-control
  deadpan). Measured pace: Spanish about 175 to 205 words per minute, English about
  140 to 150 with pauses.
- 10 to 30 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Subtitles burned in, because most
  people watch muted.
- **Never:** cartoons or drawn characters (Federico, 2026-10-31), speech-bubble
  slideshows, silent videos, paid tools, CapCut.

## What we sound like

- **Voice:** the funny friend in the group chat. Warm, fast, self-deprecating, a little
  dumb on purpose.
- **English:** deadpan, quick, meme-literate. "POV: you said 'one more episode' at 11pm."
- **Español:** exagerado, cariñoso, de barrio. "Cuando tu mamá dice 'ahorita vengo'."
  Neutral Latin American first. Venezuelan slang as a signature (gafo, chamo, pana,
  ¡qué ladilla!, hallacas, tupper), explained by context.
- **Language:** each Reel is one language and posts alternate EN and ES. A bilingual
  caption only when the video mixes both.
- **Swearing:** no.
- **Emoji in captions:** one or two, used as punctuation.
- **Face on camera:** faceless. No people on camera; bystanders only as unrecognizable
  dots or hands.
- **Hashtags:** 3 to 5, specific, at the bottom. Instagram caps them at 5.

Comedy shapes that work for us:

- **Family roles cast with animals or objects** ("La tía", "el abuelo", "el primo"),
  never nationalities or ethnicities.
- **Everyday thing explained by something huge** (a goodbye as a rocket launch).
- **Absurd exaggeration of a small moment** (65°F as a national emergency).
- **POV / "Cuando..." / "Mi familia cuando..."** with a reaction clip.
- **Bilingual misunderstandings and false friends** (embarazada vs embarrassed).
- **Recurring characters, as voices over footage** (La Tía Gafa, El Pana Experto,
  Mr. Literal, El Recién Llegado). Each has one flaw and one catchphrase.
- **A question at the end that people answer for themselves** ("¿Cuál eres tú?").

## Off limits (legal rulebook, strictest reading, no exceptions)

- **Never mock, name, show, voice or imitate a real person.** No celebrities, politicians,
  creators, private people, kids. No lookalikes, impersonations, voice clones or AI
  likenesses. No "in the style of" a living person.
- **Never compare a nationality or ethnicity to animals** (Instagram hateful-conduct
  rule). Use "mi familia", "la tía", "los primos". The word "venezolana" next to goat
  footage was sent back by legal on 2026-10-31.
- **No punchlines about** race, religion, nationality, accent, disability, sexual
  orientation, gender identity or body type. Do not mock a country's accent.
- **No defamation:** no false facts about a real person, business or brand, even as a
  joke. No accusations of crimes, affairs, illness, drugs or corruption.
- **No politics** (current events are fine, taking sides is not), no fresh tragedies,
  deaths, disasters or crimes with victims. No misleading content about elections,
  health or emergencies. Weather jokes get a same-day check for a real advisory.
- **Music and audio:** original, in-house, Meta Sound Collection, or licensed for
  commercial use with the license on file. No ripped songs, not even 5 seconds.
- **Footage:** only PD, CC0, CC BY (credit on screen and in the caption with the license
  URL), NASA (no people, no insignia as a graphic, "NASA does not endorse this video"),
  or commercial-use stock. Never CC BY-SA, NC or ND. No TV, movies, news, memes or
  other creators' clips. Reposts only with written permission (rulebook section 6).
- **No brand logos as a punchline.** Phone, chat, bank and weather screens are invented
  designs. Saying "WhatsApp" is fine; showing its logo or UI is not.
- **No engagement bait:** no "comment YES", "like if", "tag 3 friends", "share if".
  A real question people answer for themselves is fine.
- **Labels:** Instagram "AI info" label ON (synthetic voices). Sponsored posts get
  "Paid partnership" plus #ad or #publicidad. Sponsor deals go to Federico first.
